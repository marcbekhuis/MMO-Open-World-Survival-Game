# Windstep

Windstep gathers air beneath and behind the caster for one brief surge of movement. It is the foundation wind-mobility spell, extending an existing dodge, jump, or sprint without becoming unrestricted flight.

## Gameplay Role

The caster chooses a direction and is carried a short distance along it, preserving momentum while gaining enough lift to cross a gap, evade a heavy attack, reach low cover, or change elevation on broken ground. Windstep cannot pass through solid obstacles, cancel a long fall by itself, or be chained without pause. An opponent can anticipate its destination from the caster's movement and the visible pull of dust and air.

Outside combat, the spell helps a prepared traveller negotiate ravines, ruins, rooftops, and difficult climbs. It expands route choice but does not replace climbing equipment, mounts, or the effort required to reach the world's highest spaces.

## Upgrade Directions

Upgrades can extend horizontal travel, add lift, reduce recovery time, or improve directional control in mid-air. Feathered landing sacrifices distance to absorb more downward momentum. Slipstream lowers the mana cost when Windstep follows a sprint, while rescue draft allows the caster to push a nearby falling ally at reduced strength.

## Trade-Offs

Windstep commits the caster to a visible trajectory and leaves a short recovery on landing. Greater speed makes collision more punishing and demands more space to stop. Heavy equipment and carried weight reduce its distance, while severe opposing winds make it less reliable. The spell cannot be cast while fully immobilised or without room for the caster to move.
