# Gust

Gust releases a short, directional blast of air from the caster. It is the foundation wind-control spell, valued less for damage than for changing distance, interrupting exposed actions, and turning loose parts of the environment into opportunities.

## Gameplay Role

At close range the blast pushes light enemies, deflects smoke and gas, scatters loose debris, and disrupts unbraced aim. Cast downward at the right moment, it can soften a short fall or give a jump extra reach. Heavy targets, firm footing, walls, and shields reduce its force, and it cannot fling an opponent from full stability without substantial investment and a favourable position.

Gust is strongest when the caster reads the terrain. It can clear sight through smoke, fan an existing fire, move a drifting hazard away from an ally, or punish someone fighting too close to a ledge. Because it affects the world in front of the caster, careless use can spread flame or contamination just as easily as it clears them.

## Upgrade Directions

The spell can grow toward a wider fan, a narrower high-pressure burst, greater range, or improved control over falling. Crosswind bends projectiles slightly without replacing a shield. Sustained gust turns the instant blast into a brief channel that moves persistent smoke and light objects, while backdraft pulls air toward the caster before releasing it outward.

## Trade-Offs

Gust has little value against armoured or anchored opponents on open ground. Increasing force raises recoil, noise, and mana cost, and the caster can be pushed backward if they release it without stable footing. Its environmental effects are neutral, so wind that saves an ally from poison may also carry that poison toward someone else.
