# Frostbind

Frostbind draws heat from a small patch of ground and locks moisture into grasping ice. It is the foundation water-control spell, creating a temporary snare whose strength depends on preparation, terrain, and the target's response.

## Gameplay Role

The spell first chills a visible area, then slows anyone who remains within it as ice gathers around boots, paws, or lower limbs. Targets that stand still too long become briefly rooted, but movement, jumping, heat, and deliberate strikes against the ice break the bind. Wet targets and waterlogged ground freeze faster, while dry sand and hot stone resist the effect.

Frostbind is useful for protecting a retreat, holding a creature inside another spell's area, or making a narrow approach dangerous. It can also stabilise a small patch of shallow water long enough to cross or cool an overheated object, but it is not strong enough to create permanent bridges or preserve food indefinitely.

## Upgrade Directions

The caster can invest in a wider chill, faster freezing, a stronger root, or longer-lasting ice. Creeping frost allows the patch to spread slowly over wet ground. Brittle bind makes the ice easier to break but causes it to burst into slowing shards when destroyed. Deep freeze narrows the area around one target and increases commitment on both sides.

## Trade-Offs

Frostbind has low direct damage and loses value when enemies refuse to remain in its area. Stronger binds require longer casting and more obvious ground effects, giving opponents time to react. Fire removes the ice quickly, and allies are affected unless an upgrade teaches the spell to recognise the caster's group at an additional mana cost.
