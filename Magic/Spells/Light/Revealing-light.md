# Revealing Light

Revealing Light creates a slow-moving orb that illuminates a chosen area and reacts to concealed disturbances. It is the foundation light-utility spell, supporting exploration, watch duty, and detection without granting perfect knowledge.

## Gameplay Role

The orb follows the caster, remains at a marked point, or drifts forward along a simple path. Its steady light exposes footprints, disturbed dust, tripwires, fresh blood, and the shimmer of basic magical concealment within a limited radius. It does not identify what left a trace, see through solid cover, or automatically reveal a skilled hidden player; instead it makes subtle signs easier for an attentive group to read.

The spell is valuable in caves, ruins, fog, and night travel, but that value cuts both ways. A floating light is visible from a distance, announces the group's route, and can become a lure for creatures or hostile players.

## Upgrade Directions

Upgrades can increase brightness, duration, travel distance, or sensitivity to one kind of trace. Watchlight anchors the orb and causes it to flare when something crosses nearby. Veiled glow reduces its visibility at long range but narrows illumination, while true spectrum improves detection of magical residues at the cost of ordinary brightness.

## Trade-Offs

Only one orb can be maintained by a caster at a time, and stronger detection increases its ongoing mana drain. Heavy rain, dense fog, dust, and competing magical effects reduce clarity. Enemies can avoid its radius, obscure an area with enough physical material, or destroy the orb with a deliberate attack, making it a tool for careful investigation rather than an answer to every ambush.
