# Flame Wall

Flame Wall raises a short line of sustained fire across the ground. It is the foundation fire-control spell, designed to divide a battlefield, close a narrow route, or make a pursuer pay for following rather than to trap enemies without warning.

## Gameplay Role

The wall appears after a brief flare along its chosen line, giving attentive players time to step clear before the flames rise. Crossing it deals damage and applies a short burn, while creatures that fear fire may hesitate or change course. The spell is strongest across doors, bridges, passages, and the edge of an allied formation, but opponents can wait out its short duration, go around it, leap narrow sections, or use water magic to cut a temporary gap.

Flame Wall also changes the environment. It can ignite prepared oil and dry vegetation, provide momentary warmth, and cast enough light to reveal movement nearby. These uses are intentionally temporary so the spell supports survival and ambush play without replacing campfires, tools, or construction.

## Upgrade Directions

Upgrades can lengthen the wall, raise it higher, extend its duration, or improve how quickly it forms. A segmented wall allows the caster to bend the line around terrain at the cost of shorter total reach. Smouldering ground leaves a weaker hazard after the main flames fall, while controlled burn reduces the chance of spreading fire to nearby objects and allies.

## Trade-Offs

Length, duration, and heat all increase mana upkeep. The wall is highly visible, can reveal a hidden group, and is unreliable on open water or in severe rain. Poor placement may obstruct allies as effectively as enemies, and the caster cannot move an existing wall once it has formed.
