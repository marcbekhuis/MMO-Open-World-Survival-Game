# Static Field

Static Field charges a small area with restless electrical potential. It is the foundation lightning-control spell, turning repeated movement through a space into a risk without creating an invisible or unavoidable stun zone.

## Gameplay Role

The field appears as crawling sparks, lifted hair, and a rising crackle before it becomes active. A target crossing the area builds charge; rapid movement, metal armour, and wet conditions build it faster. At a threshold the charge discharges for light damage and a brief stumble, then resets. Standing still or leaving by the shortest route limits the danger, while grounded earth and insulating equipment drain charge more quickly.

Static Field protects a doorway, discourages a charge, or makes a wet patch tactically dangerous. It combines well with knockback and slowing spells, but its warning signs and delayed trigger give opponents time to choose another route.

## Upgrade Directions

The spell can grow toward a wider area, faster charge, longer duration, or a stronger discharge. Grounded perimeter concentrates the danger at the field's edge. Residual charge causes affected targets to retain part of their buildup after leaving, while selective current lowers the effect on allies at the cost of higher upkeep.

## Trade-Offs

The field costs mana to maintain and affects the battlefield indiscriminately without specialisation. Rain and shallow water make it stronger but harder to control, potentially carrying charge outside the intended boundary. Earth magic can ground it, wind does little to move it, and opponents can destroy or cover the visible focus point at the field's centre to collapse the spell early.
