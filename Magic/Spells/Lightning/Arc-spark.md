# Arc Spark

Arc Spark snaps a fast electrical discharge toward a nearby target. It is the foundation lightning attack, built around short range, quick reactions, and conductive conditions rather than the delayed devastation of advanced storm magic.

## Gameplay Role

The spark deals light damage and briefly disrupts a target's current action when it lands cleanly. It reaches farther through water, wet targets, and connected metal, allowing a prepared caster to arc the discharge to a second nearby enemy. Dry air, insulating materials, distance, and broken line of conduction sharply reduce its reliability.

Arc Spark is useful for punishing an exposed cast, interrupting a creature's minor attack, or exploiting the soaked state created by water magic. Its disruption is subject to diminishing returns and cannot repeatedly lock down the same opponent. Heavy actions and large creatures may continue through the hit even when they take damage.

## Upgrade Directions

Upgrades can extend range, strengthen disruption, reduce recovery, or improve conductive chaining. Forked spark divides power between two close targets. Capacitor stores a stronger discharge while the caster holds the spell, increasing both mana cost and the danger of interruption. Nerve strike narrows the timing window but rewards a hit during an enemy action.

## Trade-Offs

The spell's short range exposes the caster to retaliation, and conductive environments do not distinguish friend from foe without costly control upgrades. Metal equipment can improve the route to a target but also make the caster vulnerable to reflected or hostile lightning. Stronger disruption increases cooldown and produces a bright, sharp report that reveals the caster.
