# Thunder

Thunder is an advanced lightning spell that calls a storm-charged strike down onto a targeted area. It is powerful, loud, and risky: a spell that rewards setup and timing over constant casting, and the heavy answer lightning gives to the quick strikes of [Arc Spark](Arc-spark.md) and the zones of [Static Field](Static-field.md).

## Gameplay Role

Thunder is a charged, two-handed spell under the [casting rules](../../../Magic.md#casting): the caster raises both hands and gathers while holding the button, then lets go to name the place the strike will fall. They must stand still while gathering, and plain damage breaks the gathering, as does any stagger. It is an advanced spell, unlocked by the lightning school's foundation spells, [Arc Spark](Arc-spark.md) and [Static Field](Static-field.md), together with the wind school's [Gust](../Wind/Gust.md), and it asks for a minimum Spell Power on top of the ranks in all three. After the strike it carries a recovery period before it can be gathered again, a default a server may override.

The spell is best used against clustered enemies, large creatures, siege targets, or opponents forced into predictable movement. The strike deals heavy lightning damage and briefly disrupts the actions of those it catches, but it never falls without warning: the marked ground crackles and the air darkens for a moment before the bolt lands, long enough for attentive players to run clear, put cover between themselves and the mark, or rush the caster and break the gathering.

## Upgrade Directions

Upgrades change how the strike arrives and what it passes through, never simply how hard it hits. Strike speed shortens the delay between the mark and the impact, at the price of a smaller area and a clearer tell in the caster's raised hands. Conductive spread carries the strike through wet ground, metal armour, and storm weather, so that it chains to those standing near the target. Focused bolt narrows the area for heavier single-target damage. Storm call lengthens the recovery period and raises the mana cost in exchange for repeated strikes over a short span.

## Trade-Offs

Every gain in power costs mana, gather time, or exposure to the weather. In dry conditions the strike is less reliable, while rain, high altitude, and open water make it more dangerous to everyone nearby, the caster's allies included. The recovery period leaves the caster without their greatest spell for a time after each strike, the caster is rooted and conspicuous while gathering, and the thunderclap carries for kilometres and tells every creature and every watching enemy where the mage stands.
