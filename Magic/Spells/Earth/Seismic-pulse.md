# Seismic Pulse

Seismic Pulse drives a compact shock through the ground around the caster. It is the foundation earth-control spell, rewarding firm footing and close-range timing with an interruption rather than serving as a source of sustained damage.

## Gameplay Role

The pulse unsettles nearby enemies, breaking fragile actions and briefly staggering targets that are moving, charging, or poorly braced. It can stop a rush, create room around a surrounded caster, and expose cracked floors, loose stone, buried hollows, or unstable tunnel sections. Large creatures and shielded players may resist the stagger but still receive the warning tremor.

The spell travels through connected solid ground. It is strong on rock and packed earth, weaker through mud or constructed floors, and unable to reach airborne targets or enemies separated by open water. This makes positioning part of the spell rather than a cosmetic concern.

## Upgrade Directions

Upgrades can widen the pulse, sharpen its interruption, or focus it into a narrow cone with greater reach. Faultsense improves its ability to reveal unstable surfaces and shallow hidden spaces. Aftershock creates a weaker second pulse after a delay, while heaving ground adds a small displacement at a higher mana cost and with a clearer wind-up.

## Trade-Offs

The caster must plant their feet during release, leaving a readable interruption window. Wider pulses lose force at the edge and can disturb allies attempting precise actions. Repeated use is loud, may attract nearby creatures, and can destabilise already damaged terrain, so casting underground or inside a settlement carries risk.
