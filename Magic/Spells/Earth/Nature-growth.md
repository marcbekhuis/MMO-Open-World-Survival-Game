# Nature Growth

Nature Growth is an advanced earth spell that draws on the ground's own mana to hasten what the land would restore by itself. It supports gathering, settlement recovery, and ecological balance, and it is bounded on every side so that it eases the exhaustion of a place without ever manufacturing wealth from nothing.

## Gameplay Role

Nature Growth is a charged, two-handed spell under the [casting rules](../../../Magic.md#casting): the caster kneels with both palms on the earth and gathers while holding the button, standing still the whole time, and lets go to release the growth across the chosen area. Plain damage breaks the gathering, as does any stagger. It is an advanced spell, unlocked by the earth school's foundation spells, [Seismic Pulse](Seismic-pulse.md) and [Stoneguard](Stoneguard.md), and it asks for a minimum Spell Power on top of the ranks in both.

Every resource node in the area that has been worked down receives a share of the spell's strength, and each node has a resistance set by its rarity, tier, and ecological importance. Common plants and basic wood answer quickly, while rare ores, ancient trees, magical herbs, and boss-linked materials ask for much more strength than the spell can spare. The strength is divided among every eligible node within the radius, so a small cluster of basic resources may recover in full while a wide area holding rare nodes recovers only in part. A caster improves the result by choosing the place, narrowing the area, or shaping the spell through upgrades. What the spell restores is bounded by what [Gathering](../../../Gathering.md) already allows: it brings a living node back toward the state the ground would give it unaided, and a seam that has been mined out for good stays gone.

[NPC mages](../../../NPCs.md) cast Nature Growth around [great cities](../../../Structures/Great-cities.md) during peak activity to prevent early resource exhaustion. This helps new players in crowded areas while scarcity holds in remote, dangerous, and high-tier regions.

## Upgrade Directions

Upgrades change where the restoration falls and what it costs, never how much a node can ever hold. Focused growth narrows the radius so each node receives more restoration strength. Wide renewal expands the area but weakens the share each node receives. Efficient channel lowers the mana cost when the spell is cast in healthy natural surroundings. Deep roots lets the spell reach nodes of a higher tier at the price of a longer gather and a larger draw on the ground.

## Trade-Offs

Nature Growth restores a given node only once per regrowth span, a default a server may override, and never above what the ground naturally holds there, so casting it again on the same ground in the same span only wastes mana. It draws the ground's mana level down like any other spell, as [Magic](../../../Magic.md#casting) sets out, so a place worked hard with it is a place where every caster, the one who cast it included, recovers more slowly afterwards. The gathering leaves the caster kneeling and rooted in the open, and the green surge that follows is plain to see, which draws attention to a gathering party in dangerous country. Poor soil, bare rock, and ground already drained of mana weaken the result further.
