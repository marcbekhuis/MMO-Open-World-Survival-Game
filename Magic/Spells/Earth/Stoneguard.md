# Stoneguard

Stoneguard draws a fitted shell of earth and stone across the caster or a nearby ally. It is the foundation earth defence, providing a brief window of physical protection at the cost of speed and magical flexibility.

## Gameplay Role

The shell absorbs part of incoming weapon and impact damage until it breaks or expires. It is especially useful while holding a doorway, reviving an ally, surviving a creature's charge, or buying time for a slower spell. Lightning and blunt force travel through the rigid guard more effectively than blades and arrows, while sustained attacks can strip it away piece by piece.

Stoneguard needs mineral material to form efficiently. Bare rock and packed ground offer a strong, inexpensive shell; wooden floors, ships, deep snow, and open water force the caster to create more of the material through mana and reduce the spell's efficiency.

## Upgrade Directions

Upgrades can reinforce the shell, reduce its weight, shorten its casting time, or extend it to a nearby ally. Reactive plates break outward when destroyed and briefly stagger a close attacker. Anchored guard greatly improves resistance to knockback but nearly roots the protected character. Porous stone gives up some armour to reduce heat and lightning transfer.

## Trade-Offs

Stoneguard slows movement and stamina recovery while active, and its weight makes swimming or climbing dangerous. A stronger shell takes longer to form and is more conspicuous. The spell blocks only part of incoming damage and offers little protection against effects that bypass armour, so timing it well matters more than maintaining it continuously.
