# Treant Elder

Nothing about the Treant Elder is quick, and nothing about it needs to be. The largest and oldest tier of [Treant](Treant.md) is a wooden giant between 8 and 12 metres tall and heavy enough, at tens of tonnes, that the ground gives where it stands. It fights by commanding the forest floor, so an encounter with one is a fight with the terrain itself.

## Appearance and Visual Design

An elder keeps the treant body plan at a scale that no longer looks like a person: two arms, each a trunk-thick bough that hangs to its knees, and two legs like root pillars that flare at the base. Its bark is grey-brown and cracked like a dry riverbed, thick enough to hold hollows and whole shelves of fungus. Hanging moss trails from its shoulders, birds nest in the hollows of its back, and its head carries a crown of live branches with real canopy growth in season. Sap shows only in the deep-set knots of the face, which stay dark until the elder is roused.

Each step is a long, grinding shift of weight, and crossing a clearing takes it far longer than it takes a player, who can leave one behind without hurrying. It never gives chase. It stands among the giant trunks of the Ancient Forest, roots sunk, and waits. Next to trees that climb for hundreds of metres it is small, and with its limbs still and its crown of branches blending into the lower canopy, it is among the hardest creatures in the forest to pick out. Travellers have walked beneath one for an hour without knowing.

## Encounter

An elder joins the warning ladder described in [Treant](Treant.md) only at its last step, when Guardians have already engaged and the offender is still in the grove. It never strikes at people. It turns the ground. Roots rise under the offender in waves that trip, pin and crush, thickets shift and close behind and ahead to seal exits, and the forest floor re-forms into a maze that has to be fought or cut through, while the elder calls more Guardians from the surrounding trunks. A player who has learned the signs sees it coming: the leaves fall from a wide ring of trees around the elder, the ground hums underfoot, and the knots of its face flare.

There is no reliable way to win that fight, and the way to survive it is to stay out of it. The roots and thickets answer to the elder's attention, so putting distance between it and the offender ends the pressure faster than any damage. A very large, well-prepared group can cut down an elder, but it has to break the Guardians first and cross a floor that fights back the whole way, and the Elves will treat those who do it as the forest's enemies. Fire works on an elder as on any bark, at the price [Treant](Treant.md) describes, and here the price is a burning grove. An elder that falls leaves heartwood, the rarest material the forest gives up, and bark plates.

## Story Hook

A guild's careless fire has burned a corner of the Ancient Forest, and a grove that stood quiet for generations has started to move. The Elves of the nearest canopy settlement ask the players to lead the guild out of the wood before the elder that has woken turns the ground around the roots of their tree-homes, and warn that a forest which loses one of its oldest will not forgive how it was done.
