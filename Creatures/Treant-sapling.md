# Treant Sapling

A trespasser in the Ancient Forest meets the Treant Sapling before any other treant, and keeps meeting it: a wiry wooden biped between 1 and 2 metres tall and 20 to 50 kilograms in weight, which slips through the branches overhead, watches for harm done to the wood and carries the news back to the heavier tiers of [Treant](Treant.md). It is the smallest and fastest of them, and the forest's watcher.

## Appearance and Visual Design

A sapling has two arms and two legs, all of them long for the body. The arms grow from the top of a narrow, split trunk that forms the torso, the legs from its base, and every limb is flexible green wood rather than the stiff bark of the older tiers. The trunk and limbs are a fresh, pale green-brown, with lighter yellow wood showing where the bark has split at the joints. Clusters of leaves grow from the head and shoulders like loose hair, and the face is a narrow oval of knots and cracks that glows in the dark of the canopy. The hands end in thin branch-fingers that close around a bough like a monkey's, and the feet are splayed root-toes that grip bark.

No other treant moves like it. On the ground it runs in a low, loping crouch, fast enough to overtake a running player between the trunks, but it prefers the trees. It swings from branch to branch by its long arms, leaps the gaps between trunks, and stops dead in the middle of a bough with its limbs folded against the wood, where a leaf-crowned head reads as a cluster of growth on the tree. A sapling that holds still is close to impossible to find.

## Encounter

The first sign is a stir in the canopy where no wind blows, then a pair of amber glints in a knot, spaced a little too evenly to be a knot at all. This is the second step of the warning ladder described in [Treant](Treant.md): the sapling shadows the offender, keeping pace overhead and out of reach, and leaves it at that. It exists to be seen, and a player who notices it and puts down the axe, the torch or the pick is being offered the chance to walk away before a Guardian is called.

Attacked, cornered or pushed too far, a sapling fights as it moves, with quick jabs of its branch-fingers and sweeping kicks that knock a player off balance, retreating into the canopy after every exchange and returning from another side. It is fragile, and a few solid blows bring it down, but killing one counts as an attack on a treant, one of the four acts that provoke the forest. Like fire, it jumps the ladder instead of climbing it: the dying sapling's cry goes through the grove and the Guardians come at once, with none of the warnings between. Anyone who wants to be rid of a shadowing sapling does better to outwalk it out of the grove than to cut it out of the tree. Archers and mages with reach can pick one off a bough without closing distance, though the Guardians answer that just the same. A fallen sapling leaves sap resin and thin strips of bark.

## Story Hook

A harvesting crew working the rare reagents of the Ancient Forest finds, three mornings running, that the marks they cut into the trunks the day before have been healed over in the night. On the third day they see why: a pair of amber eyes in the leaves above, which has followed them since they entered the grove. The Elves of the nearest canopy settlement pay well for the reagent, but they have also seen the healed marks, and whether they trust the crew with the next harvest depends on whether it stops cutting.
