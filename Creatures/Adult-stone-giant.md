# Adult Stone Giant

Most travellers who speak of a stone giant mean this one. The Adult Stone Giant, the middle form of the [Stone Giant](Stone-giant.md), is a humanoid of rock between 20 and 50 metres tall, heavier than a thousand tonnes, with stone growths that cover it like the ridges of a slope. It is tall enough to see a caravan from kilometres away, and strong enough that only a large, organised party will challenge it.

## Appearance and Visual Design

The adult still has a humanoid frame, two arms and two legs, but the outline has begun to break up. Shoulders are broad and cliff-like, the arms carry knobs and ridges of rock grown over the forearms, and the back and shoulders are covered in rough stone growths of shale and quartz that stand out from the smoother body like scree on a mountainside. The legs and lower body are dark basalt, the torso banded in lighter strata, and the head, a blunt, heavy block, has lost most of its features to weathering. Mana crystals are large and plentiful: long violet and blue-white seams along the spine and shoulders, clusters the size of a person at the knuckles, and a bright band across the upper chest where the heart lies, high on the body and well out of reach from the ground.

It moves more slowly than a young giant, but each stride eats an enormous amount of ground, and a player cannot outrun one on open ground. The way to get away is to leave the open: steep scree, narrow ravines and broken country that the giant has to cross more carefully than a person does.

## Encounter

At this size the giant's attacks change in character. It still stomps and sweeps, but they now reach across whole ledges and bring down the slope around a fight, and its hurled boulders come in numbers and arc from a long way off. Its shockwave, struck into the ground with both fists, is the signature threat: it throws a whole party off its feet and cracks the rock beneath, so the warning signs described in the hub are the cue to get behind cover or off the ground.

Killing an adult takes planning. The heart is high on the chest, so the pickaxe route in [Stone Giant](Stone-giant.md) depends on built platforms and grappling hooks, and the giant does everything it can to knock them down. A party that climbs needs others to hold the giant's attention from the ground, and needs the platforms rebuilt as fast as they are crushed. Starvation is more practical here, but the adult's range is wide and its appetite large, so emptying it of crystal is a campaign and not an afternoon. Siege weapons work well at this size, because the giant is big enough to hit and slow enough to be hit.

Feeding an adult is a serious obligation. A settlement has to set aside a large and steady share of its crystal to keep one docile, and the settlement that pays it becomes a prize for any larger giant that follows the same crystals.

## Story Hook

A caravan route through a high pass runs beneath a ridge that has started to move. The traders who use it say an adult giant has taken to following the wagons that carry crystals, and that the guild escorting them has found it will leave a wagon alone if the crystals are dropped a day's march back. The escort wants the players to find out whether the giant can be led off the pass, or whether it has to be brought down.
