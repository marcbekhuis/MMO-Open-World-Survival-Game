## Open questions

Questions awaiting the user's direction before the design can commit. Phrase each as a concrete question, with the options or trade-offs noted where they are known.

- **Combat at an unavailable-region wall.** How should projectiles, area effects, knockback, and targets whose owner has failed behave at the restriction? Define consistent attack and outcome rules without granting invulnerability or treating stale ghosts as live targets. The approved movement and warning behavior is in [Server Architecture](Server-architecture.md#53-unavailable-destinations-and-visible-feedback).
- **Cross-server combat and durable handoff.** Define who validates hits and commits death, loot, and inventory changes during transfers, including crashes before or after cutover, uncertain acknowledgements, group membership changes, and coordinator failure. Preserve the single-owner requirements in [the technical guide](<Server-architecture (Technical).md#72-preparation-cutover-and-group-state>).
- **Client connection continuity.** Select and validate a UE 5.8.2 connection mechanism that meets the seamless handoff requirements, with a migration path to UE6. Generic packet rerouting and visually masked reconnection are insufficient evidence of continuous gameplay. See [the technical guide](<Server-architecture (Technical).md#73-client-connection-continuity>).

### Multiplayer integration review

The cross-system review on 5 September 2026 identified the following refinements required to support existing game designs. These are research and decision items, not adopted implementation choices. They extend the handoff questions above; overlap hysteresis, coordinated passenger transfers, and the visible unavailable-region wall remain settled.

- **Gameplay relevance beyond visibility.** Define separate rules for visual replication, audible or otherwise perceptible events, authoritative interactions, and persistent events between distant entities. [Audio Direction](Audio-direction.md) makes sound gameplay information; the [Mermaid](Creatures/Mermaid.md) can influence unseen listeners and appear differently to different players; the [Royal Dragon](Creatures/Royal-dragon.md) can react when its distant mate is harmed. The architecture's visibility-only synchronization rule does not cover these relationships, and the technical `RegionHasPlayers` check omits AI-only recipients. Determine the minimum information each client and server needs, including the protection of private inventory and [Cartography](Skills/Artisan/Cartography.md) knowledge.

- **World domains, dungeon topology, and streaming granularity.** Define how outdoor vertical columns, oceans, floating islands, and the impossible interiors of [Dungeons](Structures/Dungeons.md) map to simulation ownership. A dungeon can have its own sky, clock, shifting layout, and gravity, so an X/Y region key alone does not express every playable space or traversable connection. Evaluate domain-qualified region identities and explicit entrance connections while preserving the seamless-world requirement; a spatial domain does not itself imply a private party instance. Keep logical ownership regions, content-streaming cells, and replication cells independently tunable. [Epic's World Partition documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/world-partition-in-unreal-engine) describes content streaming and configurable cells; gameplay jurisdiction remains a separate design responsibility.

- **Durable item and currency transactions outside handoff.** Extend the existing death/loot question to [Inventory](Inventory.md), [Economy and Trade](Economy.md), capital banking and auctions in [Capital Cities](Structures/Capital.md), and [Crafting](Crafting.md). Define one recoverable outcome for payment and delivery, container transfers, gathering depletion, material consumption, item creation, and associated skill progress. Cover concurrent users, changed bag capacity, station destruction, and crash/retry behavior. A completed sale must not restore the seller's item after recovery while leaving the buyer's copy intact. [PostgreSQL's transaction-isolation documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html) supports investigation of concurrent durable updates; it does not by itself specify the game-server protocol.

- **Item Box, death ordering, and time.** Define when a deposit becomes protected from death, when mana is charged, and what happens to continuous drain and preservation during disconnects, outages, or exhausted mana. The [Item Box](Magic/Spells/Misc/Item-box.md) promises private, weightless storage that survives death, while [Death and Loss](Death-and-loss.md) excludes its contents from corpses. A deposit racing with death must produce one location for each item. Neither continuous online drain nor storage persistence settles the offline gameplay rule.

- **Connected structures and mobile bases.** Define authority and persistence for the connected support relationships in [Building System](Building-system.md), including a foundation and dependent components on different region servers, concurrent edits, damage, and unavailable neighbours. Extend the approved vehicle-group model to the [Flying Leviathan](Creatures/Flying-leviathan.md), whose back supports a built mobile base: placed structures, stored contents, walking occupants, and attachment changes need explicit membership and transfer rules. Also define hostile physical relationships such as a [Kraken](Creatures/Kraken.md) gripping a ship without automatically treating the enemy and ship as one friendly transfer group.

- **Spell timelines and environmental interactions.** Expand generic active-effect snapshots into a contract for shared timing, warning windows, channels, per-target counters, interruptions, and attribution. [Thunder](Magic/Spells/Lightning/Thunder.md) must warn before impact; [Mending Light](Magic/Spells/Light/Mending-light.md) charges mana against actual healing and can be interrupted; [Static Field](Magic/Spells/Lightning/Static-field.md) retains charge and changes its effect on allies. [Flame Wall](Magic/Spells/Fire/Flame-wall.md) and [Gust](Magic/Spells/Wind/Gust.md) also change environmental hazards. Resolve what continues, cancels, or commits when caster, target, and effect have different owners or one owner fails.

- **Shared clocks and unobserved world activity.** Specify full simulation, reduced simulation, and catch-up behavior for the systems that matter without a nearby player. [NPCs](NPCs.md) travel, trade, patrol, and generate consequences when convoys disappear; [Taming and Control](Taming-and-control.md) includes raising creatures and maintaining bonds; [Weather, Time, and Seasons](Weather-time-and-seasons.md) changes route access, fire, projectiles, and resources. Assign authority to shared weather, regional market state, boss-trigger history, and dungeon clock domains. Define recovery and elapsed-time rules explicitly. Offline bond neglect is already an open concern in the taming document; this review does not decide that player absence counts as neglect or add unattended crafting queues.

- **Portals, respawn, login, and disconnects.** Define a destination-reservation and readiness contract for [Travel and Mounts](Travel-and-mounts.md) portals and the bound-city, bed, and claimed-point respawns in [Death and Loss](Death-and-loss.md). These jumps bypass the ordinary overlap. Set the ordering of arrival, tolls, applicable cooldown or cargo restrictions, corpse creation, and source retirement; cover unavailable destinations and invalid respawn points. Define whether a disconnected character remains vulnerable, how one active character session is enforced, and how authenticated access under [Monetization and Platforms](Monetization.md) survives regional transfers. A region move must not become a new character login or an escape from consequences.

- **Social identity, jurisdiction, and downstream rewards.** Distinguish physical transfer groups from parties, guilds, alliances, creature handlers, and legal ownership. [Guilds and Factions](Guilds-and-factions.md) governs shared storage and negotiated access; [Frostbind](Magic/Spells/Water/Frostbind.md) can spare the caster's group; [Conflict and PvP](Conflict-and-pvp.md) promises city-guard intervention; [Reputation](Reputation-system.md), [Quests](Quests.md), and [Player Progression](Player.md) give lasting consequences and rewards. Define which membership or permission version applies to an action, how changes reach other servers, and how one combat or quest outcome yields its rewards and consequences without duplicates. A simulation-region boundary must not redefine city jurisdiction, guild access, or credit for a tame's actions.

- **Hotspot capacity and failure fairness.** Extend [Server Settings](Server-settings.md) with representative workloads drawn from capital markets, [Raids](Raids.md), naval bosses, and mobile settlements. Include building pieces, affected targets, effect fan-out, AI, physics relationships, persistence throughput, and stacked vertical spaces alongside player counts. A single overloaded region cannot distribute its internal work merely by moving intact to another server. Define acceptable degradation and admission behavior without making guard protection or essential attack warnings disappear. Decide corpse recovery-window treatment, raid objectives, and other timed opportunities during an outage; offline raid eligibility remains a gameplay choice rather than an inferred server rule.

### Cross-document discrepancies affecting multiplayer research

- **Leviathan speed.** [Server Architecture](Server-architecture.md) and [Dynamic Culling and Render Distance](Dynamic-culling-and-render-distance.md) use the leviathan as a fast-flight example, while [Flying Leviathan](Creatures/Flying-leviathan.md) explicitly makes it slow. Separate long-range visibility due to size from movement-speed look-ahead when refining relevancy.
- **Floating-island access.** [Travel and Mounts](Travel-and-mounts.md) makes flight the only access route, while [Floating Islands](Biomes/Floating-islands.md) also permits climbing and grappling. Resolve this before defining the required approach and transfer paths.
- **Item destruction on death.** [Economy and Trade](Economy.md) and [Crafting](Crafting.md) broadly count full-loot death as destroying some goods, while [Death and Loss](Death-and-loss.md) describes PvP loot as recirculating and assigns some environmental loss to corpse recovery. Clarify the intended sink and expiry rules before specifying durable item outcomes.

## Deferred decisions

Choices intentionally left open for now, each with a short note on why, so a later session does not reopen them by accident or mistake them for settled.

- **Substances stay flavour-only.** Alcohol and narcotics are world texture, not a mechanical subsystem, per [Content and Tone](Content-and-tone.md). A future pass could give intoxication real buffs, debuffs, or an addiction loop, but that is a deliberate decision to revisit, not a gap to fill in passing.
- **Disease is a hazard, not a standing meter.** [Survival](Survival.md) treats illness and wounds as conditions the world inflicts and the player treats (with the [Swamp](Biomes/Swamp.md) as the signature source) rather than as a fourth survival bar alongside food, fatigue, and exposure. Promoting disease to a full subsystem with its own progression, immunity, and cures is a deliberate future choice, left open rather than assumed.
