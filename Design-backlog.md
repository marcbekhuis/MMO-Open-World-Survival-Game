# Design Backlog

This is a working file, not part of the design proper. It carries context between sessions so that ideas, questions, and deferred decisions survive even though AI assistants do not share memory. Everything recorded here is provisional: items move out of the backlog and into the design documents as they are written, answered, or decided. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for how this file fits the wider workflow.

## Open questions

Questions awaiting the user's direction before the design can commit. Phrase each as a concrete question, with the options or trade-offs noted where they are known.

- **Keeping a PvE player's own base safe while they are offline.** Offline raiding is open ([Raids and Conquest](Raids.md)) and logged-out characters sleep in the world except in the safe areas of NPC cities ([Death and Loss](Death-and-loss.md)), so a player who wants to avoid PvP can only log out safely in a city. How should a PvE-minded player who builds their own base protect it and themselves while logged out, without creating an exploit for PvP players? Deferred by the user on 10 October 2026 for a later session.

- **Titan Turtle bases and shelter.** The [Building System](Building-system.md#bases-on-creatures) says a base on a creature needs no claim marker because its keeper holds the building rights, but the [Titan Turtle](Creatures/Titan-turtle.md) file also has towns taking root on wild shells with no keeper, and a coerced turtle goes its own way when control fails. What happens to a turtle-borne base when its keeper logs out, and who holds the rights on a wild shell? A turtle is also too large for any roof, so it cannot meet the shelter conditions: should it, like the leviathan, have a shelter rule of its own?

- **Do frames and beams carry load?** The [Building System](Building-system.md#snap-points) lets support frames, crossbeams, braces, and trims snap onto any wall face. Should they only dress a wall, or should braces and crossbeams also extend its stability, so that bracing a long run is a structural choice as well as a visual one? The text currently gives them no effect on stability. Two smaller details were also filled in by the writer and await confirmation: a placed piece's style can be changed without tearing it down, and decoration can be placed on an optional light grid.

- **An earth spell that breaks stone.** The [Building System](Building-system.md#damage-and-siege) lists earth magic as a siege tool, but no earth spell yet damages structures; [Seismic Pulse](Magic/Spells/Earth/Seismic-pulse.md) interrupts and reveals unstable ground. Add a structure-cracking upgrade to Seismic Pulse, or a new advanced earth spell in the way [Fireball](Magic/Spells/Fire/Fireball.md) serves the fire school?

- **Can anything extend breath?** Mermaid scales already feed water-breathing equipment ([Mermaid](Creatures/Mermaid.md)). Should that gear, an alchemical draught, or a spell also lengthen breath in thin air, or should the high sky stay a hard limit that no preparation lifts? Until this is decided, [Survival](Survival.md#breath) and [Flight](Flight.md#weather-and-altitude) name no way to extend breath.

- **Open implementation questions in the system technical guides.** Each of the four guides written on 11 October 2026 ends with a short list of decisions the specification leaves open but an implementation cannot avoid. They await the user's answers, and each answer belongs in the spec first and its guide second: [Building System (Technical)](<Building-system (Technical).md#11-open-implementation-questions>), [Creature Riding (Technical)](<Creature-riding (Technical).md#14-open-implementation-questions>), [Flight (Technical)](<Flight (Technical).md#15-open-implementation-questions>), and [Taming and Control (Technical)](<Taming-and-control (Technical).md#9-open-implementation-questions>).

- **Combat at an unavailable-region wall.** How should projectiles, area effects, knockback, and targets whose owner has failed behave at the restriction? Define consistent attack and outcome rules without granting invulnerability or treating stale ghosts as live targets. The approved movement and warning behavior is in [Server Architecture](Server-architecture.md#53-unavailable-destinations-and-visible-feedback).
- **Cross-server combat and durable handoff.** Define who validates hits and commits death, loot, and inventory changes during transfers, including crashes before or after cutover, uncertain acknowledgements, group membership changes, and coordinator failure. Preserve the single-owner requirements in [the technical guide](<Server-architecture (Technical).md#72-preparation-cutover-and-group-state>).
- **Iris or the Replication Graph.** Epic's [5.8 release notes](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/unreal-engine-5-8-release-notes) call the Iris replication system production-ready for licensees, while its 5.8 documentation pages still label it Experimental. Iris cannot be combined with the Replication Graph that [the technical guide](<Server-architecture (Technical).md#5-replication-graph-visibility-driven-sync>) is built on; it has its own filters, prioritisers, and fast-array type. Decide whether to stay on the Replication Graph or move to Iris, and update the server guide and the system guides that reference it.
- **Client connection continuity.** Select and validate a UE 5.8.2 connection mechanism that meets the seamless handoff requirements, with a migration path to UE6. Generic packet rerouting and visually masked reconnection are insufficient evidence of continuous gameplay. See [the technical guide](<Server-architecture (Technical).md#73-client-connection-continuity>).

### Multiplayer integration review

The cross-system review on 5 September 2026 identified the following refinements required to support existing game designs. These are research and decision items, not adopted implementation choices. They extend the handoff questions above; overlap hysteresis, coordinated passenger transfers, and the visible unavailable-region wall remain settled.

- **Gameplay relevance beyond visibility.** Define separate rules for visual replication, audible or otherwise perceptible events, authoritative interactions, and persistent events between distant entities. [Audio Direction](Audio-direction.md) makes sound gameplay information; the [Mermaid](Creatures/Mermaid.md) can influence unseen listeners and appear differently to different players; the [Royal Dragon](Creatures/Royal-dragon.md) can react when its distant mate is harmed. The architecture's visibility-only synchronization rule does not cover these relationships, and the technical `RegionHasPlayers` check omits AI-only recipients. Determine the minimum information each client and server needs, including the protection of private inventory and [Cartography](Skills/Artisan/Cartography.md) knowledge.

- **World domains, dungeon topology, and streaming granularity.** Define how outdoor vertical columns, oceans, floating islands, and the impossible interiors of [Dungeons](Structures/Dungeons.md) map to simulation ownership. A dungeon can have its own sky, clock, shifting layout, and gravity, so an X/Y region key alone does not express every playable space or traversable connection. Evaluate domain-qualified region identities and explicit entrance connections while preserving the seamless-world requirement; a spatial domain does not itself imply a private party instance. Keep logical ownership regions, content-streaming cells, and replication cells independently tunable. [Epic's World Partition documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/world-partition-in-unreal-engine) describes content streaming and configurable cells; gameplay jurisdiction remains a separate design responsibility.

- **Durable item and currency transactions outside handoff.** Extend the existing death/loot question to [Inventory](Inventory.md), [Economy and Trade](Economy.md), great-city banking and auctions in [Great Cities](Structures/Great-cities.md), and [Crafting](Crafting.md). Define one recoverable outcome for payment and delivery, container transfers, gathering depletion, material consumption, item creation, and associated skill progress. Cover concurrent users, changed bag capacity, station destruction, and crash/retry behavior. A completed sale must not restore the seller's item after recovery while leaving the buyer's copy intact. The same guarantee covers the [Item Box](Magic/Spells/Misc/Item-box.md), whose gameplay rules are now settled (protected once the deposit cast completes; drain paused offline; sealed when mana runs out): a deposit racing with death must still end with each item in exactly one place. [PostgreSQL's transaction-isolation documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html) supports investigation of concurrent durable updates; it does not by itself specify the game-server protocol.

- **Connected structures and mobile bases.** Define authority and persistence for the connected support relationships in [Building System](Building-system.md), including a foundation and dependent components on different region servers, concurrent edits, damage, and unavailable neighbours. Extend the approved vehicle-group model to the [Flying Leviathan](Creatures/Flying-leviathan.md), whose back supports a built mobile base: placed structures, stored contents, walking occupants, and attachment changes need explicit membership and transfer rules. Also define hostile physical relationships such as a [Kraken](Creatures/Kraken.md) gripping a ship without automatically treating the enemy and ship as one friendly transfer group.

- **Spell timelines and environmental interactions.** Expand generic active-effect snapshots into a contract for shared timing, warning windows, channels, per-target counters, interruptions, and attribution. [Thunder](Magic/Spells/Lightning/Thunder.md) must warn before impact; [Mending Light](Magic/Spells/Light/Mending-light.md) charges mana against actual healing and can be interrupted; [Static Field](Magic/Spells/Lightning/Static-field.md) retains charge and changes its effect on allies. [Flame Wall](Magic/Spells/Fire/Flame-wall.md) and [Gust](Magic/Spells/Wind/Gust.md) also change environmental hazards. Resolve what continues, cancels, or commits when caster, target, and effect have different owners or one owner fails.

- **Shared clocks and unobserved world activity.** Specify full simulation, reduced simulation, and catch-up behavior for the systems that matter without a nearby player. [NPCs](NPCs.md) travel, trade, patrol, and generate consequences when convoys disappear; [Taming and Control](Taming-and-control.md) includes raising creatures and maintaining bonds; [Weather, Time, and Seasons](Weather-time-and-seasons.md) changes route access, fire, projectiles, and resources. Assign authority to shared weather, regional market state, boss-trigger history, and dungeon clock domains. Define recovery and elapsed-time rules explicitly. The gameplay rules for absence are now settled — bonds hold in a stocked stable and otherwise weaken slowly without breaking ([Taming and Control](Taming-and-control.md)), and settlements repopulate on their own clock ([Settlement Generation](Settlement-generation.md)) — so what remains is applying them correctly to elapsed time. This item does not add unattended crafting queues.

- **Portals, respawn, login, and disconnects.** Define a destination-reservation and readiness contract for [Travel and Mounts](Travel-and-mounts.md) portals and the bound-city, bed, and claimed-point respawns in [Death and Loss](Death-and-loss.md). These jumps bypass the ordinary overlap. Set the ordering of arrival, tolls, applicable cooldown or cargo restrictions, corpse creation, and source retirement; cover unavailable destinations and invalid respawn points. A logged-out character now stays in the world asleep and lootable outside city safe areas ([Death and Loss](Death-and-loss.md#logging-out)), so the sleeping body is a persistent entity that needs a simulation owner, survives server restarts and region transfers, and must reconcile with the player's next login. Define that, how one active character session is enforced, and how authenticated access under [Monetization and Platforms](Monetization.md) survives regional transfers. A region move must not become a new character login or an escape from consequences.

- **Social identity, jurisdiction, and downstream rewards.** Distinguish physical transfer groups from parties, guilds, alliances, creature handlers, and legal ownership. [Guilds and Factions](Guilds-and-factions.md) governs shared storage and negotiated access; [Frostbind](Magic/Spells/Water/Frostbind.md) can spare the caster's group; [Conflict and PvP](Conflict-and-pvp.md) promises city-guard intervention; [Reputation](Reputation-system.md), [Quests](Quests.md), and [Player Progression](Player.md) give lasting consequences and rewards. Define which membership or permission version applies to an action, how changes reach other servers, and how one combat or quest outcome yields its rewards and consequences without duplicates. A simulation-region boundary must not redefine city jurisdiction, guild access, or credit for a tame's actions.

- **Hotspot capacity and failure fairness.** Extend [Server Settings](Server-settings.md) with representative workloads drawn from great-city markets, [Raids](Raids.md), naval bosses, and mobile settlements. Include building pieces, affected targets, effect fan-out, AI, physics relationships, persistence throughput, and stacked vertical spaces alongside player counts. A single overloaded region cannot distribute its internal work merely by moving intact to another server. Define acceptable degradation and admission behavior without making guard protection or essential attack warnings disappear. Decide corpse recovery-window treatment, raid objectives, and other timed opportunities during an outage. Offline raiding is allowed by design ([Raids and Conquest](Raids.md)), so an outage must not become a way to shield or expose sleeping bases unfairly.

## Deferred decisions

Choices intentionally left open for now, each with a short note on why, so a later session does not reopen them by accident or mistake them for settled.

- **Substances stay flavour-only.** Alcohol and narcotics are world texture, not a mechanical subsystem, per [Content and Tone](Content-and-tone.md). A future pass could give intoxication real buffs, debuffs, or an addiction loop, but that is a deliberate decision to revisit, not a gap to fill in passing.
- **The flying-mount roster waits for the bestiary.** [Flight](Flight.md) names only the [Flying Leviathan](Creatures/Flying-leviathan.md) and the [Royal Dragon](Creatures/Royal-dragon.md) as flyers. Adding a mid-tier working flyer (a giant cliff bird, a lesser wyvern, or both) was proposed on 11 October 2026 and deferred by the user until the creature list is further along.
- **Disease is a hazard, not a standing meter.** [Survival](Survival.md) treats illness and wounds as conditions the world inflicts and the player treats (with the [Swamp](Biomes/Swamp.md) as the signature source) rather than as a fourth survival bar alongside food, fatigue, and exposure. Promoting disease to a full subsystem with its own progression, immunity, and cures is a deliberate future choice, left open rather than assumed.
